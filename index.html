    Moving Zavala Forward

    Our mission is to enhance the quality of life for all Zavala County residents. We understand our community, who you are, what matters to you, and the future you are building. Every day, we strive to elevate the standard of our services, because we believe high expectations create opportunity, not obstacles. Through our shared commitment, resilience, and diversity, we work together to achieve the aspirations of every resident.

    Latest News

    Burn Ban

    All outdoor burning is prohibited in Zavala County from 8 July 2026 through 6 October 2026, following an order issued by the Zavala County Commissioners Court.

    New Developments and Countywide Investments

    New Multipurpose Building, Sheriff's Maintenance Warehouse, Nutrition Centers; and truck and emergency equipment.

    Subdivision Regulation

    Zavala County adopted new rules in April 2026, to guide safe and organized growth in areas outside city limits.

    Precinct Improvement Projects

    Zavala County is advancing projects across all four precincts, including street lighting, park upgrades, facility improvements, road maintenance equipment, and infrastructure enhancements in La Pryor.

    Policies and Strategic Initiatives

    Tax relief for companies to support growth and attract business. Colonia grant funding for Chula Vista, Batesville, Hacienda, and La Pryor, focused on water, sewage, and precinct needs.

    TextMyGov Election Text Alerts Available

    Stay informed with Zavala County Elections through TextMyGov. Text “Zavala Co Elections” to 80946 to receive election updates, notifications, and important information directly to your phone.
