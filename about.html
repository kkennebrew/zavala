    About Zavala County, Texas

    Zavala County, located in the heart of South Texas, is a vibrant community known for its rich cultural heritage, agricultural roots, and close-knit neighborhoods. Home to Crystal City, the “Spinach Capital of the World”, the county blends small-town charm with opportunities for growth and development.
